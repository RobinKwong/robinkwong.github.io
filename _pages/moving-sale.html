I'm moving to London at the start of November and the flat has to be empty by Halloween. Almost nothing in it is coming with me.

Everything that's going is listed further down this page. Have a scroll, put whatever takes your fancy into the basket and send me the list. I'll write back with the address and we'll find you a time. Nothing's held until I reply.

Saturday 3 October 11am – 5pm

Saturday 10 October 11am – 5pm, whatever's left

32nd Street, Astoria between 30th and 31st Avenue, a few minutes from the N and W at 30th Ave

Message me for the door number, or just ask for it when you send your list. Can't make either Saturday? Say so and we'll find another time. Most of it is free; for the handful of things that aren't, it's cash, Venmo or Zelle on the day.

The small print, such as it is

Who's asking. I'm Robin Kwong. I've lived on 32nd Street in Astoria for four and a half years and I'm moving to London at the start of November. This is my actual flat and these are my actual things.

Collection only. 32nd Street in Astoria, between 30th and 31st Avenue — about twenty minutes from midtown on the N or W, and easy enough from most of Brooklyn. I'll send the door number when you get in touch. I'll help you carry things down and out to a car, but I can't deliver.

Nothing is held until I reply. If two people want the same thing, whoever can come on one of the two Saturdays gets it, because that's the one I can actually manage.

Paying, for the few things that cost anything. Cash, Venmo or Zelle. I'll give you the details when you're standing in front of me, not on a public web page.

Come and say hello even if you don't want anything. There'll be drinks and snacks on both days and I'd rather the flat was full of people than quietly emptying.

Cats not included. Coco and Petal appear in the background of several of these photographs and are not available. They're flying to London, which they have opinions about.

Not everything is listed. The furniture is going separately, and there's a pile of things too dull to photograph. Come and look on the day.
