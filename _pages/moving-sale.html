The small print, such as it is

Who's asking. I'm Robin Kwong. I've lived on 32nd Street in Astoria for four and a half years and I'm moving to London at the start of November. This is my actual flat and these are my actual things.

Collection only. 30-83 32nd Street, Apt 1B, Astoria NY 11102 — about twenty minutes from midtown on the N or W, and easy enough from most of Brooklyn. I'll help you carry things down and out to a car, but I can't deliver.

Nothing is held until I reply. If two people want the same thing, it goes to whoever can come on one of the two Saturdays, because handing things over on the day is the only arrangement I can manage while packing.

Paying, for the half of it that costs anything. Cash, Venmo or Zelle. I'll give you the details when you're standing in front of me, not on a public web page.

Come and say hello even if you don't want anything. There'll be drinks and snacks on both days and I'd rather the flat was full of people than quietly emptying.

Cats not included. Coco and Petal appear in the background of several of these photographs and are not available. They're flying to London, which they have opinions about.

Not everything is listed. The big furniture is going separately, and there's a pile of things too dull to photograph. Come and look on the day.
